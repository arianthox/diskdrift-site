Privacy policy

Local by design.

Effective October 16, 2026

Summary

DiskDrift does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app operates locally on your Mac and does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data collection SDKs.

Folder access

DiskDrift can access only folders you explicitly select using the macOS folder picker. It uses that access to scan visible regular files, calculate local storage summaries, and perform cleanup actions you explicitly confirm.

Security-scoped bookmarks may be stored locally so DiskDrift can reopen folders you selected or run monitoring jobs while the app is active. These bookmarks and their folder paths remain on your device.

Information stored on your Mac

DiskDrift may store your selected-folder bookmark, monitoring preferences, scan schedule, and recent monitoring summary in its macOS application container. This information is used only to provide app functionality and is not transmitted off your Mac.

You can remove monitored folders inside the app. Uninstalling DiskDrift and removing its application container removes its locally stored settings.

File cleanup

DiskDrift never deletes files automatically. When you approve a cleanup action, the app revalidates the selected item and moves it to the macOS Trash. DiskDrift never empties Trash; permanent deletion remains a separate action performed by you in Finder.

Network access and third parties

DiskDrift does not require an account and does not send your file names, paths, metadata, scan results, or usage information to the developer or to third parties. Apple may independently process App Store purchases, downloads, and diagnostics under Apple’s own privacy policies.

Children’s privacy

DiskDrift does not knowingly collect information from children or from any other users.

Policy changes

If DiskDrift’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released. The effective date above will also be revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the DiskDrift public support tracker. Please do not include private file paths, file names, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
