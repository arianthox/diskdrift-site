DiskDrift support

Help with storage review on your Mac.

DiskDrift supports macOS 14 or later. It works locally and requires no account.

Open a support request

DiskDrift cannot read a folder

Choose the folder again using DiskDrift’s folder picker. If access still fails, check macOS System Settings under Privacy & Security and confirm the folder or volume is available.

A monitored folder stopped scanning

Remove the monitored folder and add it again. This refreshes the security-scoped permission stored by macOS.

What happens during cleanup?

DiskDrift rechecks each approved item before moving it to Trash. It does not permanently delete files and never empties Trash.

Where are scheduled scans run?

Monitoring runs locally while DiskDrift is active. Enabling “Start DiskDrift when you log in” allows the app to resume monitoring after sign-in.

How do I review moved files?

Use “Review Last Moved” or “Open Trash” in DiskDrift. Finder handles review, restoration, and permanent deletion.

Before reporting a problem

Include your macOS version, DiskDrift version, and the steps that caused the issue. Do not post private paths, file names, screenshots containing personal data, or files from your Mac.
